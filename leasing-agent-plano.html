Frequently Asked Questions

Leasing Services in Plano, TX — FAQ

What is tenant placement (leasing-only) service in Plano?

Tenant placement means we find and qualify a tenant for your Plano rental, then hand the property back to you to manage. It covers rent pricing, marketing, showings, screening, the lease and move-in. If you would rather not self-manage, we also offer full-service Plano property management.

What is the difference between leasing-only and full property management?

Leasing-only ends once the tenant moves in. Full property management continues after move-in with rent collection, maintenance coordination, renewals, inspections and monthly owner statements through the Rentec Direct owner portal.

How do you screen tenants?

Every applicant is reviewed against the same written tenant selection criteria — credit, verified income, rental history, references and background — applied consistently under fair housing laws. Texas law requires landlords to make their selection criteria available to applicants, and we provide them with every application.

How do I rent a home in Plano through Axial Realty?

Tell us your budget, move-in date, preferred schools and pet needs. We search MLS rental listings across Plano, schedule showings, and help you submit a complete application. When you find a home we manage, you can apply online.

Do renters pay a fee to work with you?

In most leases we handle, the property owner pays the leasing fee. If we represent you as a tenant and a fee applies, it is disclosed in writing before we begin. Standard application fees for screening may apply.

How soon must a Texas landlord return a security deposit?

Under the Texas Property Code, a landlord generally must refund the security deposit, or provide a written itemized list of deductions, within 30 days after the tenant surrenders the property — provided the tenant has given a forwarding address.
